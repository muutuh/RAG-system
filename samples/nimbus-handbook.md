# Nimbus Coffee Co. — Employee Handbook

*This company is fictional. The handbook exists to test the RAG system: an LLM cannot know these facts unless it reads them here.*

## Working hours

Our roastery in Harrowgate opens at 6:30 and closes at 19:00. Baristas work in two shifts: the early shift runs from 6:00 to 14:00 and the late shift from 12:00 to 19:30. The overlap between 12:00 and 14:00 exists so the lunch rush always has double staffing.

Every employee gets a 40-minute paid break per shift. Breaks cannot be taken between 7:30 and 9:00, which is the morning peak.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 23 days of paid vacation per year, plus the company-wide closure week between Christmas and New Year. Part-time employees receive vacation in proportion to their contracted hours.

Unused vacation days can be carried into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Anything above 5 days expires on March 31st.

Sick days do not need a doctor's note for the first 3 consecutive days. From the 4th day onward, a note is required.

## Coffee allowance

Every employee may drink unlimited filter coffee during their shift. In addition, each employee receives a monthly allowance of 2 kilograms of roasted beans to take home. The allowance does not roll over to the next month.

## Equipment and uniform

The company provides two aprons and one pair of anti-slip shoes per year. Shoes can be replaced earlier if they are damaged at work; talk to your shift lead.

The Marzocco espresso machines are cleaned by the closing barista every night using the blind-basket backflush procedure. Grinder burrs are replaced every 900 kilograms of coffee, which the head roaster, Ines Okafor, tracks in the maintenance log.

## Training

New baristas complete a 3-week training programme before working a shift alone. The programme ends with a practical exam: pulling six espresso shots within 2 grams and 3 seconds of the target recipe.

Employees who pass the advanced cupping course receive a one-time bonus of 350 euros.

## Contact

Questions about pay go to the payroll team at payroll@nimbus.example. Questions about schedules go to your shift lead.
